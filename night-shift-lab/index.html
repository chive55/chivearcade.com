Night Shift Lab

A hospital lab sim with light horror, played in first person. You're the only tech on the 0400 to 0700 shift for five nights. Run the specimens, follow the rules sheet (it changes every night), and report what's wrong with the lab.

In development · PC · Made in Godot · Chive Arcade

About the game

  • Five nights on the 0400 to 0700 shift. You get a new rules sheet each night, and night 5 has three endings.
  • Lab work comes first. You run daily QC, take in tubes, work the analyzers and the lab computer, call in critical results and crossmatch blood.
  • 17 room anomalies and 13 specimen anomalies, plus rejected samples, critical values, crossmatches and bad QC.
  • Something stalks you from night 2 on. Early on, keep the flashlight on it. On nights 4 and 5, turn the light off.
  • The shift escalates from Settling In to Restless to Surge, and if the dread builds too high, your shift ends.
  • Full controller support, plus save and continue, and settings.
  • Overtime opens up after night 5. It's endless waves with no 07:00 cutoff.
