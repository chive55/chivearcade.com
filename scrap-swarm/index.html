Scrap Swarm

An arena survival game where you last 10 minutes against waves of junk robots, level up as you go, and weld two maxed weapons into a stronger one.

In active development · PC · Made in Godot · Chive Arcade

About the game

  • 3 bots: Scrapper, Tinker and Volt.
  • 8 weapons, plus 6 welds that fuse two maxed weapons into a stronger one.
  • 8 synergy overdrives that push a build further.
  • 3 arenas: Junkyard, Foundry and Storm Yard.
  • Elites and bosses show up late in a run.
  • The Workshop has 9 upgrades you buy between runs.
  • Difficulty goes from Rookie up to tier 4.
