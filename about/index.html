About Chive Arcade

Chive Arcade is an indie studio, and Vroom Boom is our first game. It's an arcade car combat game where you ram, boost and fire missiles at the other cars. You can fight AI bots in battle royale, hold out in wave survival, or play two player split screen with a friend. Wishlist it on Steam.

Right now we're building Vroom Boom and sharing progress as we go. There's more coming from the studio after that.

Questions or press: mail@chivearcade.com.
