About the game

Vroom Boom is an arcade car combat game from Chive Arcade. Pick a car, load it up with rockets, mines and missiles, and go. Take on a pack of bot cars in battle royale, or hold out as long as you can in wave survival. Team up with a friend in 2 player co-op, or go head to head in local split screen.

Key features

  • Battle royale A pack of bot cars, one arena, and the last car running wins.
  • Wave survival The waves keep getting harder. See how long you last.
  • 2 player co-op Team up with a friend.
  • Local split screen 2 player versus on one screen.
  • Weapons Rockets, mines and missiles, so you can fight your way.
  • Boost and jump Get out of trouble, or jump right into it.
  • Arenas and garage Fight across several arenas, and earn more cars as you play, each with its own special.
  • Online play Planned for the full game.
