Vroom Boom is arcade car combat coming to Steam in 2027, and the game is still in development. The idea is simple and loud: fight with rockets and mines, take big jumps, and keep moving. It is built around short, readable bursts of chaos rather than careful, cautious driving.

A useful tip is to learn the car you are in, not just the opponent in front of you. You earn more cars as you play, and each car has its own special, so the right move in an online 1v1 can change with the car you bring. Knowing what your current special does gives you more to work with than rockets alone.

That earning loop is a core design choice. Progress does not only mark another win, it changes the tools you take into the next fight, which keeps rockets, mines and jumps from playing the same way every time. Play Vroom Boom here: https://chive-arcade.itch.io/vroom-boom
