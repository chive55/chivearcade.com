Arcade car combat

Pick a car, load it with rockets, mines and missiles, and drive. Vroom Boom is the first Chive Arcade game, and it's coming to Steam. Lots of cars to earn, each with its own special. Gameplay

What you do in Vroom Boom

  • Online play: 1v1, Free For All, King of the Hill and Capture the Flag.
  • Battle royale: a pack of bot cars, one arena, last car running wins.
  • Wave survival: keep going as the waves get harder.
  • 2-player co-op: team up with a friend.
  • Rockets, mines and missiles: pick how you want to fight.
  • Boost and jump: get out of trouble, or jump into it.
  • Multiple arenas to fight in.
