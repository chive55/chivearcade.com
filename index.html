What you do in Vroom Boom

  • Online 1v1: you against one other driver.
  • Battle royale: 8 to 12 bot cars, one arena, last car running wins.
  • Wave survival: keep going as the waves get harder.
  • 2-player split-screen: two players on one screen.
  • Rockets, mines and missiles: pick how you want to fight.
  • Boost and double jump: get out of trouble, or jump into it.
  • Six arenas to fight in.
